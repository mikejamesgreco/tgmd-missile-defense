TGMD Help

Mission

Protect the neighborhood bank, dive bar, and casino from incoming Molotov cocktails. Each surviving location has its own wiseguy defender.

Controls

Click or tap anywhere in the sky. The nearest surviving defender fires a three-round burst toward that point. Bullets travel through the scene and destroy a bottle on contact.

Molotovs

Bottles arrive from above with burning rags and choose one of the surviving venues as a target. If a bottle reaches its destination, the venue is knocked out.

Labels

TGMD draws a built-in cartoon wiseguy on the bottle label when no images are loaded. Use Load Faces to select local images and TGMD will randomly use them instead. Click a loaded image to crop it.

Rounds and Waves

Ammunition is limited each wave. Surviving venues earn a wave bonus. Difficulty increases as the waves continue.

Privacy

Selected images are decoded locally in the browser. TGMD does not upload them or store them in localStorage. Reloading or closing the page removes them from the current session.
