TGMD Help

Mission

Protect the cities and interceptor batteries from incoming missiles. Click or tap anywhere in the sky to launch an interceptor toward that location. Incoming missiles carry a visible spark trail, and the interceptor explodes when it arrives, creating an expanding blast radius.

Chain Reactions

A threat destroyed inside an explosion creates a smaller secondary explosion and may give a faint browser-generated "ouch." Those explosions can destroy additional threats and create chains, increasing your score quickly.

Ammo and Waves

You begin each wave with limited interceptor ammunition. The next wave starts after every incoming threat has either been destroyed or reached the ground. Surviving cities and unused ammunition award bonus points. Difficulty increases with each wave.

Face Missiles

Use Load Images to select one or more local images. TGMD randomly places those images on incoming missile heads. Click a loaded image to open the circular crop editor, then drag the photo beneath the circle and use Zoom to frame exactly the face or image area you want on the missile head. The Face Size slider changes the size of the finished circular head during gameplay.

Privacy

Selected images are decoded locally in the browser. TGMD does not upload them or store them in localStorage. Reloading or closing the page removes them from the current session.

Controls

Start Game begins a new game. Pause freezes and resumes play. Mouse and touch input are both supported.
